01About

I'm a CS + Math senior at Duke who builds AI systems and ships products. I made Marrow Health, an iOS app that reached 4,000+ users in seven weeks, and spent the summer building agentic developer tooling at MetLife. I'm looking for new-grad software and AI engineering roles starting in 2027.

I like problems where math, infrastructure and product meet. Most of what I build starts as something slow, manual or fragile and ends as something reliable that people use every day: an email forward that replaced a planner's upload routine, an agent that turns a ticket into a pull request, an app that hands people their own health data.

This year I shipped Marrow Health solo, from the SwiftUI screens to an OAuth 2.1 relay that lets ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini read Apple Health data. Before that I built agentic tooling and LLM red-team environments at MetLife, rebuilt Azure integrations at Dexter Axle, and modeled PJM wind generation for Duke's ALIGN initiative.

Teaching keeps my fundamentals sharp. I have TA'd four Duke courses in discrete math, probability and multivariable calculus, and won the CS department's Outstanding UTA Award. Before Duke I competed in math leagues across Illinois.

I also love games. I took Duke's FOCUS cluster on virtual worlds and games, studied game design and now algorithmic game theory, and my Live Más scholarships were awarded for game design and applied math.
